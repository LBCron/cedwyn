Flywheel: Disclosure results flywheel

Cadence: proposed: every 6 hours, after email ingest (not yet scheduled)
Goal prefix (duplicate check): DISCLOSURE RESULTS
Runner: the disclosure results runner on the project box (on the project workspace)

Purpose

Use the dartboard's outcome panel as performance data so that positive returns from bodies rise month on month: learn which request features get answers, put preservation first in every draft, find new recipients from the site's own content and add new ideas each cycle.

Inputs

  • the dartboard record of every conversation and its outcome
  • new replies since the last cycle
  • unsent hive drafts

Outputs

  • feature tables (recipient type, statute, named reference, date window, preservation opener)
  • lessons with counts and examples
  • a preservation-first audit of unsent drafts
  • at least five new ideas per cycle, each marked untried, tried, adopted or rejected

Metric

share of settled requests (sent at least 28 days ago) with a positive or useful return, against a monthly target that is raised when beaten twice running

Report rules

Nothing routine; milestones ride the morning brief.
