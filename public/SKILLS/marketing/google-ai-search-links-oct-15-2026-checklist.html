Google AI Search link changes of 15 Oct 2026: what to check on our pages

Primary source read in full (4 Nov 2026): Google, "More ways to connect with the web from Search", Hema Budaraju (VP Product Management), 15 Oct 2026 blog.google . Practitioner read: Screpy, "What Changed in Google AI Search Links in October?" (Mustafa Aydemir, 19 Oct 2026) screpy.com . Tracker: keywordseverywhere.com (15 Oct entry: no click data from Google yet; Google told SER "additional metrics over time").

What Google announced (status matters: some launching, some tests)

  1. Inline links (links on words inside AI Mode / AI Overview answers): "continued and substantial increases"; now launching to show them HIGHER in responses more often.
  2. Official pages: a TEST of a dedicated section at the top of an answer for the one or two official places to complete a task, with context on why each link is useful.
  3. Timely-article carousels: rolling out to more locations and languages (none named); the carousels highlight the user's Preferred Sources. Google: Preferred Sources users up "more than 7x" since January (no base given).
  4. End-of-answer related-article links: more prominent, with image thumbnails.
  5. Creators: TEST (in AI Mode, from that week) of clickable creator/publisher names on video results that open their Google Search profile.

No traffic numbers were given. Screpy's point: a more visible link is not proof of more visits; record "this query, this device, this market, this date, this format" as the evidence, and count a click to a Search profile separately from a click to the website.

How to do it

  1. Pick 10-20 queries where we should be the source (brand, place names, the book titles, task queries like "buy X e-book").
  2. Check each in AI Mode and with AI Overviews from a UK location; screenshot/log format seen (inline link high/low, carousel, thumbnail link, official section, creator name) with date.
  3. Inline-link and thumbnail readiness: every target page needs a crisp title that answers the query, a strong og:image/thumbnail (the end links now carry images), and a clear first paragraph.
  4. Official-page readiness: for task queries ("buy", "contact", "visit"), make sure ONE canonical page does that job and states so in its title/meta; do not split the task across near-duplicate pages.
  5. Carousels favour Preferred Sources: keep the "add us as a preferred source" prompt (see google-preferred-sources-button-deeplink.md) on news/blog pages.
  6. Video creators: check whether the channel is eligible for a Google Search profile and claim it; videos then link to the profile from AI Mode.
  7. Measure in Search Console (AI features are folded into Web search totals; no separate report yet) plus analytics referrals; compare 28-day windows before/after 15 Oct, and treat any change as correlation only.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Task query: "buy the Great House Farm e-book" type searches should resolve to /ebooks/ only; CEDWYN ONPAGE run can check that /ebooks/ title/meta states the task plainly (markup-only fix; body text = PROPOSAL).
  • Thumbnails: the auto-blog and research pages need a real og:image each (end-of-answer links now show images); ONPAGE audit already checks share cards, so add "og:image present and >=1200 px wide" to its checklist (PROPOSAL).
  • YouTube channel: check Search profile eligibility for the Cedwyn channel (read-only check; claiming may need the owner's Google login = item for the problems queue if it needs him).
  • Add a 10-query UK AI Mode format log to the weekly VISIBILITY CHECK (PROPOSAL), dated, so we can see when our pages appear as inline links, carousel items or official links.
  • No claim in public text that Google features us; editorial rules apply.
