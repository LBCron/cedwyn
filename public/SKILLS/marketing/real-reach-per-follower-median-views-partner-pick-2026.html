Real reach per follower: judge accounts and partners by median recent-post views, not followers

Harvested 7 Nov 2026 (skills harvest). Topic gap: the index had gifted copies, collab posts and IG Stories reach, but no rule for picking which accounts to court or for benchmarking our own reach per follower.

The data (Acurrate "Real Reach Report", October 2026, read)

Vendor data from a creator-marketing tool's own catalogue (516,717 IG + TikTok accounts; 229,298 with measured recent posts; updated 18 Oct 2026). Not independently checked; the vendor sells this metric. The method is stated and sensible: Real Reach = median views of recent posts; Reach% = that / followers; TikTok cleaned of pinned/viral outliers.

Median reach of a typical post, share of followers: | Tier | Instagram | TikTok | |---|---|---| | Nano <10k | 53.1% | 34.8% | | Micro 10k-100k | 25.3% | 6.2% | | Mid 100k-500k | 17.2% | 3.5% | | Macro 500k-1M | 15.3% | 3.1% | | Mega >1M | 14.1% | 2.7% | (July 2026 edition, IG nano 42.4%, TikTok nano 35.3%: same shape.)

  • Instagram median engagement rate falls with size: nano 2.31%, micro 1.02%, mid 0.70%, macro 0.66%, mega 0.56% (85,826 creators, 13 Oct 2026).
  • Person beats brand on Instagram: individual creators out-reach brand-run accounts 2.0x and out-engage them 3.2x; on TikTok raw reach is level (brand 5.1% vs individual 4.3%) but individuals still out-engage 1.4x.
  • Big views are not always engaged: ~13% of IG accounts with 1M+ median views have engagement under 0.5%; on TikTok 1.3%.
  • Within any tier the spread is huge, so judge each account by its own numbers, never by the tier average.

How to do it

  1. For any account (ours, a partner, a creator to send a gifted copy to): open the last 9-12 posts, take the MEDIAN views (not the mean, not the best). Reach% = median views / followers. Engagement = median (likes+comments+saves) / median views.
  2. Rank candidates by median views per post first (absolute audience), then by engagement; drop anyone with high views and engagement under 0.5% (passive or bought).
  3. Prefer person-led accounts (a named local historian, a walker, a librarian who posts as themselves) over institutional brand accounts for collab posts and gifted copies: 2x reach on IG by this data.
  4. Benchmark our own account against the nano row (53% on IG): if our median post reaches far less than half our followers, the followers are stale or the content misses; fix content before chasing followers.
  5. Log it in a sheet: handle, followers, median views, reach%, engagement, date read. Re-read monthly; numbers drift.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • @cedwynestate and the Facebook page are nano accounts; the IG FOLLOW run grows followers. Add one check to the weekly growth report: median views of the last 9 Reels / followers, against the 53.1% nano benchmark, so follower growth is read beside reach (followers from mass-follow-back can be low-reach).
  • Gifted-copy and collab shortlist (gifted-ebook-copies-history-creators-uk-cma-asa-disclosure.md rules still apply: disclosure, no payment talk): pick Welsh/Glamorgan history creators in the 1k-50k range by median views, person-led first; a 3k-follower local historian at 50% reach (~1,500 views) beats a 60k heritage brand page at 15%.
  • Instagram Collab posts: invite person-led accounts to co-author (the brand-vs-individual gap is the reason); our side stays the brand account and never names family members.
  • TikTok: follower count is a weak signal there (nano 35%, micro already 6%); judge TikTok partners by median views only.
