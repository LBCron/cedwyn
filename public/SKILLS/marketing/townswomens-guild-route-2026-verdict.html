Townswomen's Guild (TG) speaker route — 2026 verdict: low yield, guild-by-guild only

Checked 5 Nov 2026 (skills harvest). Lead carried from the 08:38Z run.

What the record shows

  • National body: Townswomen's Guilds, charity 1115974 (Charity Commission). Year to 31 Jan 2025: income £208,610, expenditure £315,127 (spending ~£106k more than it took in). 6 trustees, 50 volunteers. The register lists a run of closed local guilds passing their funds to it (Ecclesall 2021 … Fleet (Morning) 13 Nov 2025).
  • National HQ closing: Melksham Independent News, 27 Jan 2026 — the national TG "prepares to close its headquarters at the end of the month with local Townswomen's Guilds able to continue to operate independently."
  • Same article, numbers from a guild that closed: Melksham TG (founded 1955) ended in Jan 2026 at "about 10 or 11 members". Chair Tricia Hoy: "We had to hire a hall and have a speaker once a month and only having 10 members, we weren't able to do that anymore." Friday-afternoon meetings didn't suit working women.
  • Our area: THE SOUTH WEST WALES FEDERATION OF TOWNSWOMEN'S GUILD (charity 1095570, which covered Carmarthenshire, Swansea, Pembrokeshire and the Vale of Glamorgan) was removed from the register on 6 Jan 2026 ("does not operate"). Last income £3,863 (year to Mar 2024). So no federation speaker list now covers Cardiff or the Vale.
  • Surviving guilds still book speakers monthly. Stamford TG (Lincs) meets on the 2nd Tuesday and has talks on 10 Nov 2026. A Dorset guild (Canford Heath) has its own "speaker secretary" (Facebook group thread). Speaker CVs list TG bookings next to Probus and Inner Wheel (e.g. steveroberts.org.uk: Barton TG, 4 times).
  • Wikipedia's "~30,000 members, 706 branches, 77 federations" is out of date. Don't quote it.

How to work it (if at all)

  1. Don't pitch a TG federation in South Wales: the Vale one is gone. Don't write to national HQ: it is closing.
  2. Only route: individual surviving guilds, through each one's programme or speaker secretary. Find them via the guild's own site or Facebook page, or a local community directory. Book 6–12 months ahead, because programmes are set yearly.
  3. Expect small rooms (10–30 people), a fee or travel expenses at most, and no selling at the meeting. Leave a QR card pointing to the free sample and the mailing list (same pattern as the WI file).
  4. Ask for one memory or photograph per talk (opt-in form). With older audiences, that is worth more than sales.
  5. Package the talk once and reuse it across WI, u3a, Probus, Inner Wheel and TG: same 40-minute talk, same QR card, same UTM per group (utm_source=, utm_medium=talk, utm_campaign=speaker-circuit).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Priority: LOW. The WI (wi-federation-speaker-list-audition-route.md) and u3a (u3a-speaker-circuit-local-history.md) routes reach far more people in the Vale. Add a TG guild only if it is within reach of Penarth, Cardiff or Barry and turns up in a directory search. Whether any guild survives in Cardiff/Vale is not checked.
  • Talk title in our voice: "Tŷ Mawr (Great House): a manorial house and former court house in Llandough". Never "a farm" in our own words; "Farm" only when quoting a record title.
  • Any approach is outreach: draft it as a HubSpot task only. It needs the Executive to give the talk and to say GO. Never send.
  • Stop re-harvesting this lead. Recheck only if a Wales TG federation reappears on the Charity Commission register.
