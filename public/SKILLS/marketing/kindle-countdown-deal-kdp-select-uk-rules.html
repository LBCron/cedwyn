Kindle Countdown Deal (KDP Select) — UK rules and how to run one

Date: 2026-11-04 · Topic: e-book launch/relaunch price promotion on Amazon.co.uk.

Rules (Amazon KDP Help, read 2026-11-04)

  • Only Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk; only Kindle e-books enrolled in KDP Select for ≥ 30 days (so day 31 after enrolment at the earliest).
  • List price must sit in £1.99–£15.99 incl. VAT (UK) / $2.99–$24.99 (US).
  • List price unchanged for 30 days before and 14 days after the deal.
  • Up to 5 price increments; each step down at least £1.00.
  • Duration 1 hour to 7 days, one continuous run; schedule ≥ 24 h ahead (in practice set it 2 days ahead).
  • If the Select term is NOT set to auto-renew, no Countdown in its last 14 days (WWM).
  • One Countdown or Free Book Promotion per Select enrolment period, not both (WWM; KDP lists them as the two options).
  • The page shows the regular and promo price plus a countdown clock; "you will earn the selected royalty rate on each sale during the promotion" (KDP), i.e. the 70% rate holds even at 99p (WWM).
  • KDP Select = exclusivity for the e-book edition (no other e-book retailer, including our own site's sale of the same e-book file) for each 90-day term.

Practitioner notes (Written Word Media, "Kindle Countdown Deals Explained")

  • Typical pattern: start at 99c and step the price up over the days of the deal back to list (or one tier for the whole run).
  • Main money benefit: at 99c an author normally earns 35%; under a Countdown they keep 70% (about 2x per sale).
  • Default start is 8 am Pacific; set an earlier start for UK readers (08:00 PT = 16:00 UK).
  • Amazon runs a dedicated Countdown deals page (browse by price, featured deals, email alerts) showing the struck-through regular price and the clock.
  • Publishing.co.uk 2026 guide (secondary, not checked): 500–3,000 sales when a Countdown is paired with an accepted BookBub-class promo; without paid promo expect far fewer.

Steps

  1. Decide Select vs wide. Select locks the e-book to Amazon for 90 days; for a heritage collection sold on our own site, Google Play and Apple, Select usually costs more than it gives (see amazon-kdp-2026-price-band-select-vs-direct.md).
  2. If Select: keep the list price fixed from day 0; on day ≥ 31 schedule the deal (UK market, start 00:01 UK or earlier PT hour).
  3. Price path: 99p days 1–2 → £1.99 days 3–4 → back to list. Each step ≥ £1.
  4. Line up the readers BEFORE day 1: newsletter (launch-week-email-sequence-deadline-last-day.md), Facebook/Instagram posts, the street team (reader-street-team-opt-in-launch-circle.md). assertions in copy must match the clock (uk-dmcc-act-urgency-reviews-price-copy.md: the deadline is real here, so "ends Sunday" is honest).
  5. Watch KDP real-time reports daily; after the deal, keep the price fixed for 14 days.
  6. Log units per day vs the previous 14 days in the project workspace

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The KDP account is one of the steps waiting on the project lead (not new). Until it exists this is reference only.
  • Our standing model is wide + direct (own site, Google Play, Apple, D2D). Countdown requires Select exclusivity, which would conflict with selling the same e-book on the site. Proposal if KDP is ever used: enrol ONLY a separate Kindle-only edition (e.g. a short "chapter collection") in Select and run one Countdown per 90 days at 99p→£1.99; never enrol the main collection.
  • Do not combine with a switchback test on the KDP price (the 30/14-day freeze rules it out); switchback tests run on the own checkout only (switchback-price-test-single-channel.md).
  • No money words in the Argentina scope; Countdown is UK/US only anyway.
