Organic down? Benchmark against matched peers, then grow email and Direct

Harvested 2026-11-04. Topic: analytics / funnel / email.

The evidence (primary, read)

Kevin Indig, Growth Memo, "What channels grow when organic search shrinks?" (part 2 of his 26 Oct 2026 Similarweb study; US 8,658 and France 7,730 sites with complete estimates, Sep 2023-Aug 2026). - Aug 2024 -> Aug 2026, fixed sites: organic visits -13.2% US, -23.4% France (note: an earlier lead quoted -24.4% for France; the memo text reads -23.4%). Direct -3.4% US, -5.3% France over the same period. - Size split (part 1): median small site's organic roughly flat; median large site -17.6% US / -26.0% France. Direct: median small site +14.4% US / +21.2% FR; median large -13.0% / -7.1%. - Publishers lost ~42% of estimated organic visits in both samples (via State of AI Marketing write-up of part 1). Small US sites: combined organic +19.3% but median only +2.7%; removing the top 100 small-site gainers turned combined growth negative -> always report median AND combined. - Email: median site grew email traffic +14.7% US / +17.7% France; on 1,896 shared domains email +20.5% US / +17.6% FR. Indig: email is "the least volatile acquisition and retention channel there is" and "severely underrated". - Direct is the biggest channel: median share 43.5% US / 39% FR. Indig's tactic: split homepage entrances by next page (product, pricing, login, support, content) and make the most common destinations one click away. - Indig on benchmarks: they must "fit your industry and market"; match country and period, write down why each peer belongs. - Caveats: Similarweb estimates, provisional industry labels, not representative national samples; email gains partly transactional mail.

How to do it

  1. Build a peer set of 5-10 sites in the same niche and country; write the reason for each in a file and freeze it.
  2. Use a free estimate source for peers (Similarweb free tier view, Search Console for yourself). Compare your YoY organic with the peer MEDIAN, and with Indig's size-group median (small sites ~flat). Only then attribute a drop to AI.
  3. Put the freed time into owned audience: one signup offer per page, a welcome sequence, a regular send.
  4. Direct: read homepage entrances -> next page; promote the top 2 destinations to the first screen.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Cedwyn is a small site: Indig's data states flat organic is normal for small sites; growth has to come from email and Direct/brand. Supports keeping the Mailchimp signup on every page (onpage PLAN) and the e-book welcome email.
  • PROPOSAL (growth report): freeze a 6-site UK heritage/local-history peer list (e.g. county archive, local history society, heritage trust sites) in growth-report/peers.md with reasons; record our organic YoY beside the peers' estimated median monthly. Read-only, zero spend, contact nobody.
  • PROPOSAL (ONPAGE): from Cloudflare paths, find the top 2 pages people open after the home page; if /ebooks/ or /movie/ is among them, make sure it is a first-screen link on the home page (markup only).
