Review bursts reach AI answers in days, but only where there's headroom (Indig/G2 June 2026 + Discovered Labs counter-read)

Sources: Kevin Indig for G2, "How Long Does it Take for Software Reviews to Become Visible in AI Answers?" (10 Jun 2026; read in full). Indig, "Do more G2 reviews mean more AI visibility" (Oct 2025; LinkedIn post and G2 summary, snippets only). Counter-read: Liam Dunne, Discovered Labs, "Do G2 reviews change what AI thinks of you?" (10 Oct 2026; read in full). All figures are vendor or agency data, not checked. Indig is a paid G2 advisor and G2 sells reviews; Discovered Labs sells content work. Both are B2B software, not books.

What the data states (not checked)

  • Speed (G2): 2,514 products with a review burst (5+ real reviews in 14 days), tracked 12 weeks against ChatGPT/Perplexity/Apple/Google citations. Among products whose citations responded: first lift median 4 days (25% within 1 day, 75% within 12, 90% within 31). Sustained lift settles at about 3 weeks.
  • Headroom decides size (G2): a burst lifts the odds of a citation bump 43% to 50% (+6.9 pts) vs matched controls. Under-cited products: +11.6 pts (52% vs 41%). Well-cited ones: -8.7 pts (no short-term gain).
  • Volume sets the ceiling (G2): 0 to 500+ reviews moves a free product's median AI citations "more than 800x". In the Oct 2025 study, 10% more reviews in a category meant ~2% more citations, and reviews explained <2% of the variance (G2 calls it association, not cause).
  • Counter-read (Discovered Labs): star ratings barely track how models describe brands (correlation 0.030 across 921 brands). In a 291,607-citation ChatGPT re-measure, review sites were 2% of pages read and 6% of pages cited: rarely fetched unless the prompt asks for reviews, usually kept when fetched. Their read: reviews are a retrieval asset for review-led questions, not a memory lever. What moves citations more: passage structure, entity clarity, and the same assertions repeated across independent sources.

The method

  1. Decide whether you're under-cited (the AI probe rarely names you) or saturated. Bursts only pay off for the under-cited.
  2. If under-cited, aim for a cluster of genuine reviews in a 14-day window (the study's threshold was 5+), not a trickle. Never incentivise, swap or write them.
  3. Measure on the right clock: probe review-led prompts on day 0, day 4-7 and day 21. Judge at 3 weeks, not after 1 day, not after a quarter.
  4. Only review-led prompts ("is X worth it", "X reviews", "best books on Y") should move; generic questions won't. Track them separately.
  5. Keep collecting after the burst; volume raises the ceiling.
  6. Put the rest of the effort into owned passages and consistent third-party facts. That's the Discovered Labs counterweight, and it's covered elsewhere in the index (citation source mix, consensus + information gain, Verified Source Pack).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Status: 0 sales, 0 public reviews, no Goodreads/StoryGraph listing yet (see goodreads-storygraph-listings-ebooks.md; listing is still PROPOSED). Cedwyn is maximally under-cited, which is the headroom case where a burst should matter most, if the B2B pattern carries to books (not checked).
  • Source of reviewers without a sale: the free v0.0.1 edition of each e-book. Readers of the free edition can review honestly. Plus the existing ARC/LibraryThing Early Reviewers routes already in the index. Don't build a new channel.
  • Plan for the first burst (PROPOSED; outward steps need the project lead's OK): once a book page exists on Goodreads/StoryGraph, send a single plain ask to everyone who took a free or paid copy, all inside one 14-day window. No incentives.
  • Measurement: add a "review burst" row to the weekly visibility check with dates d0 / d+4-7 / d+21 and the 3 review-led prompts already proposed in review-profiles-ai-receipts-absence-signal.md (reuse them). A citation of a review page counts; a mention in a generic answer doesn't.
  • Expectation to set: small. Even G2's own data puts reviews at <2% of visibility variance. Reviews are mainly buyer trust on /ebooks/; AI lift is a bonus on review-led questions.
