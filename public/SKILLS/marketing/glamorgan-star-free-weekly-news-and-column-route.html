Glamorgan Star — free Vale/Bridgend weekly: community-news and history-column route

Checked 5 Nov 2026. The paper's own site returns 403 to our box, so every figure below comes from search snippets of the paper's own pages and accounts (not opened directly).

What the record shows

  • Free printed local paper launched as a start-up during Covid (artytype.co.uk portfolio snippet). It bills itself as "THE VALE'S LARGEST PRINTED..." newspaper (puzzle-page snippet).
  • Reach: "over 33,000 audited readers per printed edition" (X @GlamorganStar bio; Instagram bio: "independently audited"). Its submit page: "can help get your story out into the community and reach over 33,000 readers. Submit your News, Sport, Letters and photos."
  • For comparison, Reach's paid Glamorgan Gazette lists 4,524 readers (Reach Solutions brand page). By its own figures, the free sheet's print audience is ~7x larger.
  • It already runs local-history material. A columnist announced "my new column in The Glamorgan Star" on the "legends, folklore and history of the Vale of Glamorgan and of Bridgend county" (Wales Point-to-Point FB group). It also covered a Barry-founded local book publisher seeking funding (glamorganstar.co.uk article title).
  • It carries public notices and a full free digital edition (X bio).
  • Local view (Bridgend FB group thread): some readers ask whether free papers only print news when paid. So send news that stands up on its own, not an advert in disguise.

How to work it

  1. Use the submit form (glamorganstar.co.uk). Send one news item, not a pitch deck: a 250–350 word story, one strong photo (yours, with a credit line), and a named contact.
  2. News hook first, product second: a dated event (a free talk, a new open archive page, an anniversary), then one line on where to read more. One URL with a UTM: utm_source=glamorganstar, utm_medium=print, utm_campaign=. In print, make it a short path or QR code.
  3. Letters page = a second, cheaper door: a 150-word letter answering a local-history piece.
  4. History column = the long play: offer the existing columnist a fact or record image for a future column (credited), rather than competing with him.
  5. Measure: UTM clicks in the 14 days after the issue. If it gets under 10 clicks, don't repeat that hook type.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Best-fit hook: a free local-history talk date, or a new public record page on the site, written neutrally. Name: "Tŷ Mawr (Great House), the manorial house and former court house in Llandough". Never "a farm" in our voice. Llandough Farm is a different place.
  • No case argument, no named individuals, no banned words. A news item about the heritage, not the litigation.
  • Press only on the Executive's GO: draft the submission as a HubSpot task, never send. The press/PR rules in our editorial memory apply.
  • Whether the paper takes paid-for "advertorial" only for business stories is not checked. Ask nothing about payment, and pay nothing.
