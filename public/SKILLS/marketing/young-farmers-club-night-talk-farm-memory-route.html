Young Farmers' Clubs club-night talk + farm-memory route (Glamorgan YFC)

Harvested 2026-11-05 (skills harvest). Topic: community building / rural audience; oral-history leads more than direct sales.

What the channel is (read)

  • Glamorgan YFC (county federation; presidents listed from 1939) lists its clubs at glamorganyfc.com (read 5 Nov 2026): Bridgend, Gelligaer, Gower, Llantrisant, Maendy, Neath, Pennard, Three Crosses, Wick (9 clubs). Wick is in the Vale of Glamorgan.
  • Clubs meet weekly (e.g. Bridgend YFC, since 1942, Monday 7.30pm; Gelligaer, formed 1940, Monday nights) and say they provide "tours, talks and training". Members are aged 10 to 28 (Gelligaer's page).
  • Wales YFC runs a Festival of Public Speaking (Welsh and English; debating, after-dinner speaking, reading); next date 18 April 2027 (yfc.wales, read). County rounds feed it, so clubs look for talk/reading material in winter.
  • Glamorgan YFC publishes a yearbook and a long officer history: clubs care about their own past.

Honest fit verdict

  • Low buying power (10-28), so not a sales channel. The value is: (a) a club-night talk that reaches farming families across the county; (b) older members, parents and past officers who remember farms, tenancies and Welsh-language farm life; (c) material for the movie and the academy (who farmed where, how it looked), always with consent.
  • Cost: an evening and travel. Priority: below WI federations and U3A for sales; above them for farm-memory leads.

Playbook

  1. Pick the 2-3 clubs nearest the farm's area first (Wick, Llantrisant, Bridgend).
  2. Offer a 20-30 minute club-night talk, "How a Glamorgan farm looked 100 / 200 years ago, from the records", with a short quiz (clubs like competitive formats) and old maps on screen.
  3. Offer a reading passage (bilingual if possible) that members can use as a practice piece for the public-speaking "reading" class: a free, cc-licensed extract builds goodwill and puts the text in front of judges and parents.
  4. Close with a memory ask: a simple form ("did your family farm in Llandough/Leckwith? what do you remember?"), opt-in, no names published without written consent.
  5. Leave a QR card to the free sample + mailing list (UTM utm_source=yfc&utm_medium=talk&utm_campaign=<club>).
  6. Log: talks given, attendees, memory forms returned, signups.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Needs the Executive: someone must give the talk; approaching a club is an outward contact, so draft the offer as a HubSpot draft task only and wait for his GO. Never contact individuals listed on club pages; use the club's published club address only, and only after GO.
  • Prepare now (no GO): a 1-page talk synopsis, a 2-minute reading extract from the free sample (Welsh + English), the memory form (consent wording, private storage), the QR card.
  • Editorial rules: heritage story only, no case/legal material, no accusations, nothing about family holdings or addresses; memories are private research leads, not public assertions.
