WI federation speaker list: audition route for a heritage talk (Glamorgan)

Harvested 2026-11-05 (skills harvest). Topic: community/offline audience building, talk circuit -> e-book awareness.

Why this channel

  • Women's Institute federations each keep a vetted speakers list / speakers book that local WIs book from for their monthly meeting. Getting onto it puts one talk in front of many WIs without cold pitching each one.
  • Glamorgan Federation of WIs: 87 WIs, 3,280 members as at October 2026 (glamorgan.thewi.org.uk home page, read via search snippet only: the site sits behind a browser check, not checked directly).
  • Local history is a standing category: the Staffordshire Federation Speakers Book 2023/24 (PDF, read) lists "History" across 2 pages beside Arts, Food, Travel etc., and keeps a separate Facebook list of speakers for Zoom talks.

How the gate works (primary federation pages, read)

  1. Auditions / selection days. Hampshire CFWI (7k+ members) runs five Speaker Selection Days a year (four daytime, one evening); members pay a ticket, listen to auditioning speakers and vote on who goes into the HCFWI Speakers List; application form is in the federation's monthly magazine (hampshirewi.org.uk, read).
  2. Surrey Federation: "New Speaker Auditions", members "judge and vote for their favourite prospective speakers to join the SFWI Yearbook"; tickets £5 per session / £9 all day (surreyfedwi.org.uk, read); 2026 session on 22 Apr 2026 at £7 half-day (TicketSource snippet, not opened).
  3. Gwent federation (next door to Glamorgan) ran 3 speaker workshops in 2026 (gwent.thewi.org.uk snippet; page behind a browser check, not checked).
  4. Booking mechanics once listed (Staffordshire book, read): secretaries book by email or post; they confirm title, date, start time and length, audience size, equipment; they are told to ask the speaker's current fee and travel expenses; reminder sent 2 weeks before.
  5. Selling rule: Staffordshire: "We do not recommend that speakers should sell items at WI meetings. If unavoidable, then ten per cent commission on sales should be paid to the WI." Plan for this: the talk is awareness, not a stall.
  6. Fees: a forum thread (downsizer.net, snippet only, older, not checked) gives "we generally pay 40-45" per talk. Treat as a rough floor; federation lists carry the speaker's own fee.

Playbook (steps)

  1. Find the Glamorgan Federation's speaker route: the federation office or its monthly "Glamorgan News" (PDFs on thewi.org.uk) usually announce selection/audition sessions. Ask which month the next one is and what the form wants (title, 1-paragraph synopsis, fee, travel radius, Zoom yes/no).
  2. Prepare ONE 40-45 minute talk with one strong title (a place + a question), plus a 10-minute audition cut (auditions are short slots).
  3. Offer both in person and Zoom (Staffordshire keeps a separate Zoom list; Zoom widens reach beyond Glamorgan to other federations).
  4. State the fee plainly on the form (or "travel expenses only" in year one to get booked; raise once rebooked).
  5. At meetings: no table sales; hand out a small card with a QR to the free sample chapter and the mailing list; if a WI asks to buy, follow the 10% rule.
  6. Ask each WI secretary for a written line of feedback (Hampshire has a "Speaker Feedback" form on its site) and quote it on the next application to other federations.
  7. Track: bookings per month, attendees, QR scans (UTM utm_source=wi&utm_medium=talk&utm_campaign=<wi-name>), signups, sales within 14 days.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Candidate talk: the history of a Vale of Glamorgan manorial farm through its records (tithe map, estate surveys, Welsh-language farm life before ~1970). Keep it to the heritage story; no case/legal material, no accusations, nothing about family holdings, neutral wording (editorial rules).
  • Needs the Executive: someone has to give the talk (in person or on Zoom), and the fee/expenses line is his decision. Do not apply or contact the federation without his GO (outward step). Draft the application as a HubSpot draft task only.
  • Assets to prepare now (no GO needed): the 10-minute audition script, a 1-paragraph synopsis, a QR card PDF pointing at the free sample (internal Cedwyn link with UTM), and a slide list drawn from the evidence catalogue images already public.
  • Sequence: Glamorgan first (local relevance), then Gwent and the Zoom lists of other federations.
  • Sibling routes to check later: Townswomen's Guild, Merched y Wawr (Welsh-language; already in the index), U3A (already in the index).
