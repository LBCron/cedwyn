LinkedIn content that AI search cites: articles 500-2,000 words, original knowledge posts, person + Page (Semrush x LinkedIn, 2026)

Skill harvested 2026-11-04. Topic: LinkedIn growth / AI visibility.

What the source shows (with numbers)

Semrush, "We Analyzed 89K LinkedIn URLs Cited in AI Search" (Margarita Loktionova, 10 Mar 2026). Method: 325,000 prompts (Jan-Feb 2026) across ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode and Perplexity, in 12 industries. 89,000 unique LinkedIn URLs were cited, with extra data from LinkedIn. - Reach: LinkedIn was the #2 cited domain, in 11% of AI answers on average (ChatGPT Search 14.3%, AI Mode 13.5%, Perplexity 5.3%), ahead of Wikipedia, YouTube and the news sites in their set. - Echo: AI answers closely mirror the cited LinkedIn text (semantic similarity 0.57-0.60, against Reddit 0.53-0.54 and Quora 0.435). So the first lines of a post shape how the topic gets explained. - Format: - Articles were 50-66% of cited LinkedIn content and feed posts 15-28%. - The most-cited article length was 500-2,000 words. The most-cited post length was 50-299 words. - ~95% of cited posts are original; reshares were about 5%. - 54-64% of cited posts share knowledge or practical advice (almost two-thirds on AI Mode). Promotional posts come second. - Author: - Most cited posts had moderate engagement (15-25 reactions). Virality was not needed. - ~75% of cited authors posted 5+ times in 4 weeks, and nearly half had over 2,000 followers. - Person vs Page: Perplexity cites Company Pages most (59% of its LinkedIn citations). ChatGPT Search and AI Mode cite individual members 59% of the time.

Caveats: Semrush sells AI-visibility tools and LinkedIn co-supplied data. The prompts lean towards B2B and tech, so heritage and history prompts are likely under-represented. These figures are correlations, not tests.

How to do it (steps)

  1. Write one LinkedIn article (500-2,000 words) per topic people actually ask about. Use a question or plain-topic headline, a 2-3 sentence direct answer at the top, dated facts, then the detail.
  2. State the core message in the first lines, in the exact terms you want repeated. AI paraphrases LinkedIn closely, so vague openers get echoed vaguely.
  3. Make feed posts original and 50-299 words. Explain one thing, share a first-hand finding, or document a result. Don't count reshares as output.
  4. Keep a steady cadence of 5+ posts per 4 weeks. Consistency correlates with citation more than reactions do.
  5. Use both a person and a Page. Individual posts matter for ChatGPT and AI Mode, and a Company Page for Perplexity.
  6. Measure: add 2-3 LinkedIn-relevant prompts to the AI visibility check and log whether a linkedin.com URL is cited, and whose.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The saved login is "LinkedIn — Cedwyn posting". LinkedIn is not a connected app; posts go through the browser, within LinkedIn's terms. Posting under the existing posting identity follows the current social rules: never name the project lead in public text, no banned words, no accusations about named people, nothing about family holdings.
  • Highest-value move: a LinkedIn article version of an existing answer-first page, not a link post. Examples:
  • "What was Great House Farm (Ty Mawr), Llandough? A dated timeline"
  • "Tithe maps for Welsh parish research: how we read the 1843 Llandough and Leckwith apportionments"

700-1,200 words, the short answer first, catalogue IDs, and one internal link to the matching cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate page at the end.

Publishing a new article on a new channel is an outward step. Queue it as a PROPOSAL in the project workspace for the project lead's OK, unless LinkedIn posting is already within a standing rule in the bizdev BRIEF. - Feed posts: a knowledge post of 50-299 words drawn from each new evidence row (book flywheel, gap finder), e.g. "an 1891 list of Usk burgesses names two Leckwith men; here is what it does and doesn't show". Original text, no reshares. Aim for 5+ posts per 4 weeks. - Company Page: an existing proposal (linkedin-company-page-small-heritage-org.md) needs the project lead to create it. This study adds a reason: Perplexity cites Pages 59% of the time. - Visibility check: add the prompt "who has researched the history of Great House Farm Llandough" and log any linkedin.com citation.
