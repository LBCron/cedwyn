Week 3 "Pairings" post for Nonfiction November: choosing the Wales history book to pair with Cedwyn

Harvested 2026-11-05 (lead from the last run: a Wales history book pairing for 17 Nov). It finishes the proposal in nonfiction-november-2026-weekly-themes-own-channel-pairing.md: that file gave the date and the format, this one gives the pick and the post.

The pairing format (how practitioners do it)

  • Week 3 of Nonfiction November 2026 (16-22 Nov) is "Pairings": put a nonfiction book beside another book (fiction or nonfiction) that shares a theme, so a reader who liked one picks up the other. StoryGraph even has a year-long "Fiction/Nonfiction Pairing Challenge 2026" (14 participants, 124 books, search snippet 2026-11-05) built on the idea.
  • The pairing works because the well-known book carries the reach: readers search for, rate and recognise the famous title, and the lesser-known title rides beside it.

Candidate anchor books (ratings read on Goodreads search results, 2026-11-05)

Book Ratings Avg Why / why not
John Davies, A History of Wales (Penguin) 731 3.87 The standard one-volume history and the most-rated Wales history book; broad, so the pairing line is "the big picture, then one parish up close". Recommended.
Gwyn A. Williams, When Was Wales? A History of the Welsh (Penguin, 1985) 89 4.14 Higher rating, famous question-led book tied to a TV series; fewer readers. Good second slide.
R. R. Davies, The Age of Conquest: Wales 1063-1415 not counted On the Goodreads "welsh-history" shelf; medieval only, so a narrower fit. Not checked further.

Pick: Davies as the anchor, Williams as the optional second. Both are respected, still in print and easy to find, which matters more than the rating.

The post (own channels only: Instagram @cedwynestate and the Facebook page)

  • When: Tue 17 Nov 2026, 17:00 UK.
  • Slide 1: "Nonfiction November: Pairings. Read the big picture, then one place up close." Two covers side by side (our cover; for the other book, a plain text card with title and author rather than its cover image, to avoid image rights).
  • Caption shape (about 60 words): one line on what Davies covers (Wales from the earliest times to the present), one line on what the Cedwyn e-book covers (one farm and estate south-east of the River Ely, through its records), a "fits the StoryGraph 'root' prompt" line, the free first edition link with utm_campaign=nonfictionnov2026&utm_content=pairing.
  • Tags: at most 5: #nonfictionnovember #nonfictionnov #welshhistory #localhistory #wales.
  • Kill rule: under 10 link clicks in 7 days, no repeat next year in this format.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • This is a PROPOSAL row for the queue (the project workspace) and the growth notes. It posts on our own channels within the existing auto-social caps, so it needs no outreach. Tagging or mentioning the other book's author or publisher counts as outreach and needs the Executive's go-ahead.
  • Never claim Davies's book supports anything in ours. The line is "read alongside", nothing more.
