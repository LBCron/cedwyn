## Purpose

An internal evidence-readiness index for a possible freestanding fraud action under *Takhar v Gracefield Developments Ltd* [2019] UKSC 13. It is not a court-prescribed points test, legal advice, probability estimate or finding of any court.

## Four non-compensatory Takhar elements

Each element is scored 0–50:

1. Conscious and deliberate dishonesty — a specific dishonest act, statement or concealment, not mere error or negligence.
2. Attribution to the successful party (or a person for whom it is responsible) and actual deception of the court.
3. Operative cause and materiality — the fraud was an operative cause and would have changed how the original court approached the evidence.
4. Admissible proof, provenance and particulars sufficient to establish the first three elements.

The internal floor is 25/50 on **each** limb. The floor is met only if all four reach 25. A raw total alone cannot pass a lead. The displayed progress percentage is the weakest limb divided by 25, capped at 100%; the raw sum /200 is a separate evidence index.

## What 200 means

200/200 means 50/50 on every element: twice the internal minimum on every limb. This is the owner's double-lock target, not a literal 200% probability or guarantee. A legal outcome cannot be made mathematically certain by a points scale. Independent counsel must test the pleaded case, evidence, admissibility, defences and procedure.

## Separate CPR 52.30 gateway

Reopening the final Court of Appeal decision is different from bringing a freestanding *Takhar* fraud action. CPR 52.30 requires reopening to be necessary to avoid real injustice, exceptional circumstances making reopening appropriate, and no alternative effective remedy. The authorities also require the earlier process to have been critically undermined, a powerful probability of significant injustice and a powerful probability that the decision would have been different. Fresh evidence or a merely arguable error is insufficient.

The availability of a freestanding fraud action may affect CPR 52.30's "no alternative effective remedy" condition. The route, limitation (including any deliberate-concealment issue), standing, parties and service remain unresolved; the case-wide route gate therefore blocks filing.

Reasonable diligence is not a general prerequisite to a freestanding *Takhar* fraud action where fraud was not alleged at the original trial, although deliberate failure to investigate a known suspicion may be relevant to abuse of process. Diligence is tracked separately, not scored as a *Takhar* limb.
