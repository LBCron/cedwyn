Dear Associated British Ports,

I am writing first to ask that your organisation keeps, and does not destroy, delete, weed or transfer out of its control, any record it holds that concerns Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth (beside St Dochdwy's Church), including the names and references in the P.S. below. This covers paper files, registers, plans, photographs and electronic records, and any disposal log entries about them. Where a retention schedule would otherwise lead to disposal, I would be grateful if it could be paused for these records while my enquiry is open, and for a short confirmation that this has been noted.

Having asked that, my enquiry is as follows.

I am researching Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, beside St Dochdwy's Church in Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and the land between it and the River Ely. If you consider that the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 apply to these records, please treat this as a request under them; if not, I would be grateful for your help with it as an ordinary enquiry.

Please could you tell me whether ABP holds, and if so provide copies of or the references for:
1. land plans, deeds or conveyance registers of the Penarth Harbour, Dock and Railway undertaking for land in the parish of Llandough, 1880-1990 (the Session 1881 bill gave notice of works "wholly situate in the parish of Llandough");
2. the land acquisition and reclamation records for the Windsor Dock, Cardiff scheme (Session 1898) on Llandough Moors and the River Ely diversion, 1897-1905, including the names of vendors;
3. any later sale, lease or wayleave records for land in Llandough parish that came from those undertakings, 1948-1990.

A short list of what exists would be a useful first step.
