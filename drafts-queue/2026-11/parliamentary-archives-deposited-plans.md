SUBJECT: Deposited plans and books of reference: Llandough-juxta-Penarth entries, 1843-1898

Dear Parliamentary Archives team,

I am writing first to ask that your organisation keeps, and does not destroy, delete, weed or transfer out of its control, any record it holds that concerns Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth (beside St Dochdwy's Church), including the names and references in the P.S. below. This covers paper files, registers, plans, photographs and electronic records, and any disposal log entries about them. Where a retention schedule would otherwise lead to disposal, I would be grateful if it could be paused for these records while my enquiry is open, and for a short confirmation that this has been noted.

Having asked that, my enquiry is as follows.

I am researching the history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr in the parish of Llandough-juxta-Penarth (also written Llandough juxta Cardiff), Glamorgan. Four private bills gave notice of works or land purchases in that parish:

a. Taff Vale Railway Bill: notices of November 1843 (London Gazette issue 20280, page 3747) and 21 January 1845 (issue 20434, page 176), including abandonment of part of the authorised line in Leckwith, Llandough and Penarth and the resale of land bought for it;
b. Penarth Harbour, Dock and Railway Bill, Session 1881: notice of 23 November 1880 (issue 24905, pages 6045-6046), works "wholly situate in the parish of Llandough";
c. Great Western Railway Bill: notice of 25 November 1887 (issue 25761, page 6446), the Penarth curve railway ending in Llandough-juxta-Cardiff;
d. Windsor Dock, Cardiff Bill, Session 1898: notice of 23 November 1897 (issue 26913, page 6720), acquisition and reclamation of lands in Llandough juxta Cardiff.

Please could you tell me:
1. whether the deposited plans, sections and books of reference for each of these bills survive in your holdings, with their catalogue references;
2. whether each book of reference includes entries for the parish of Llandough, and if so how I may see those entries (parcel numbers, descriptions, and the owners, lessees and occupiers listed) — for example as images, or in your search room;
3. whether any petition, minutes of evidence or committee papers for these bills name landowners or occupiers in Llandough.

If it would help to narrow the search, the farm stood beside St Dochdwy's Church, Llandough, near Penarth.
