| v0.3.0 | 2026-11-02 | Old chestnuts. The passages that raise why a record was not disclosed sooner (set aside as out of scope, released weeks later on a new request, found after a "not held" answer, produced only on challenge) now carry a crescendo highlight, a bold peach-to-red gradient stronger than the 😮 pink, in the cards, the table and the details. They form the page's own list: 🌰 bullets, each headline in quote marks with its body and date, and a "details" link to a modal with the full extract, the source, the timeline with the gap in days and weeks, and why it matters. Tapping a record on the radar opens the same details, headline in quote marks. Detection is automatic: a rule list in `_ui/chestnuts.rules.json` (with a fallback copy in toc.js) scores every clause, and a manual override list covers individual records. The app moves to /online/that-old-chestnut/ because it is not Patagonia-specific; the old /argentina/ address answers 301. |

Data-driven: a new disclosure row gets it automatically; a record can set `disclosure: true/false` to override. |
