# Changelog
- v0.3.0 (6 Nov 2026): own data store. data/central.json holds the catalogue fields (id, kind, title, date, status, page) for the record IDs in data/sources.json (EV-0631, EV-0630, AR-0002, AR-0004), pulled read-only from the central catalogue by tools/sync_central.py every 6 h (.github/workflows/sync-central.yml). records.js lists them in the Sources dialog with links to https://bucklerfamily.estate/catalogue/; the page reads only its own store. Served at https://bucklerfamily.estate/argentina-3d/ from this repo's main. — Sion's Tab agent
- v0.2.0 (4 Nov 2026): Tŷ Mawr rebuilt from three reference photographs (aerial from the SE, house front, stables from the yard): house with stone gable-end bay, slate roof, three chimneys (east one red brick), gabled porch; attached red-pantile range; lime-washed stable range with green doors and dressed-stone barn with gabled cart porch to the west; dry-stone walls, gravel yards, field gate; church to the NE; poplar windbreak. Laid out as photographed (front to the south). Views: Helicopter (from SE looking NW) and Ground (looking NNW at the stables). Procedural pantile and brick textures. — Sion's Tab agent
- v0.1.0 (4 Nov 2026): first version. Separate Patagonia world: flat steppe with tussock grass and low shrubs, real sun and sky for Puerto Madryn at the chosen hour, haze; Tŷ Mawr (footprint from the 1974 survey; walls, roof, chimneys, porch and front windows from album 096/098), north wing, east additions, walled front garden, stable range and cart shed, threshing barn, stable row, entrance gate. Sources dialog marks evidence vs best guess. — Sion's Tab agent
