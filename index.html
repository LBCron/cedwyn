Negative Finds

A record of bounded searches that did not identify a matching record or relationship. Each entry states the question, what was checked, the retrieval stage, the result, its limits and the next action.

A negative search is not proof of absence. A catalogue description, OCR transcript, full text and inspected page image are different retrieval stages; each entry keeps that distinction visible.
