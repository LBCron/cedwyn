Negative Finds

A record of bounded searches that did not identify a matching record or relationship. Each entry states what was checked, the retrieval stage, what the result does and does not establish, and the next action.

A negative search is not proof of absence. A catalogue description, OCR transcript, full text and inspected page image are different retrieval stages; each entry keeps that distinction visible.
