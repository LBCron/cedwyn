    About this diagram, the parcel numbers and what is still unsettled

    Tŷ Mawr, Llandough. Into the circle comes one farm, A + B, on the root title chain. Out comes A + B again, but as synthetic A3 and B, plus two more simple lines, SA1 and SA2, each born from a permissive occupancy ploy 💩. Press ↻ to watch every line grow and hear it told; tap anything for its record.

    Numbers are the parcel figures on the 1824 Stewart plan of Cedfin (Tŷ Mawr). The clearest figures run 1 to 45. Several figures towards the bottom of the plan read two ways — 30 to 39, or 50 to 59 if the leading digit is a 5 rather than an old-style 3; the hand cannot be settled from the photograph we hold, so both readings are listed and no number is dropped. The alternate readings cover 50, 52 and 53; no figure clearly reads 46 to 49 or 51, and that too stays open until the original is re-read at higher resolution. Pick a number for everything the records tie to it.
