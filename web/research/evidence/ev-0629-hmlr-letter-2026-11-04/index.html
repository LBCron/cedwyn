What this letter answers

The letter says it is replying to the seven numbered questions in the 12 August 2026 correspondence. It gives a direct answer to question 2: the “question or doubt” statement is said to be in the first-registration application Form 1E. For the remaining numbered points it refers back to the list of retained documents available for inspection.

That is a partial answer to the August enquiry. Whether the list answers the specific survival and item-number questions must be checked against the list itself and the underlying documents; this letter does not repeat that list or map each response to each factual sub-question.

What it does not answer in the 15 October correspondence

The 15 October 2026 letter separately asked for the notices and service records associated with the 1983 first registration of WA240304. Its section 3.6 requested the title, addressee, address, date and proof of delivery for each notice. The 4 November letter acknowledges the 15 October correspondence but responds by referring to the earlier alteration/indemnity position and to the Court. It gives no notice date, addressee, address, dispatch method, delivery record or returned-mail record for WA240304.

Finding: the notice-and-service question remains unanswered by the correspondence supplied. This does not establish that no notice was issued; the letter does not disclose whether a notice was issued or whether a service record survives.
